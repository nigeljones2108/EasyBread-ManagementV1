Commission only, a share of your net earnings. Never a retainer or an upfront fee — if you don't grow, we don't get paid.

No. There's no lock-in, and you can pick only the services you need. Leave anytime — your exact split is agreed in writing before you sign anything.

Who actually manages my account?

A dedicated, in-house team — including native English-speaking chatters. We don't outsource to low-quality teams.

Is EasyBread LGBT+ inclusive?

Yes. We're an Australia-based, LGBT+ inclusive agency, and discretion is the baseline of how we work with every creator.
