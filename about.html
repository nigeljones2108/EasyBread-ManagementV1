About us

An Agency Built By Creators, For Creators

EasyBread Management is an Australia-based, LGBT+ inclusive agency helping creators grow their OnlyFans and social media presence without giving up control of their business.

Our story

Started by creators who felt the gap

We saw too many talented creators either burning out doing everything themselves, or handing over full control to agencies that treated them like a number. EasyBread was built to be the alternative.

We're a small, hands-on team that treats your account like our own reputation is on the line — because it is.

Our approach

Flexible support, not a fixed package

You choose the services you need and skip the ones you don't. No lock-in bundles, no cookie-cutter strategy — just the support that actually moves the needle for your account.

We stay transparent about what's working, what isn't, and why, so you're always in the loop on your own business.

Our values
